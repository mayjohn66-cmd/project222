Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: standalone transparent PNG title-logo candidate for a Korean cozy fantasy shop-management game. The attached image is a PALETTE, WORLD and PIXEL-ART STYLE reference only; do NOT reproduce its background or convenience-store building.
Main request: create a carefully designed readable Korean game title logo saying exactly "숲너머 편의점".
Text (verbatim): "숲너머 편의점". Six Hangul syllable blocks in one horizontal line: 숲 / 너 / 머, space, 편 / 의 / 점. Preserve all syllable shapes accurately, especially 숲 and 의. No additional lettering, no English, no subtitle, no watermark.
Visual style: warm handcrafted pixel-art signage typography suited to the attached forest and small modern convenience store. Large clear cream/parchment colored Korean letter faces, tidy deep brown outlines and restrained chestnut-colored downward extrusion/shadow for contrast against bright green grass. Subtle warm ochre highlights. Letterforms are friendly, sturdy and easy to read at 380–460 pixels wide in a 1152x648 title screen. Consistent stroke thickness and restrained stepped pixel edges; no tiny fragmented pixels, no excessive stone cracks, no chrome bevels, no glowing outline. The title should feel welcoming and gently fantastical rather than combat-oriented.
Ornament: only a small cluster of green leaves at the upper-left edge and one modest wood/leaf flourish at the lower-right, outside the actual glyph strokes. Decorative supports may subtly suggest a wooden shop sign but do not put the title on a giant opaque rectangular plank; transparency must surround the glyphs and their ornament. Do not cover or replace any Korean letter strokes with leaves.
Composition: one centered horizontal title logo, entire silhouette visible, generous transparent padding of at least 8 percent on every edge, compact ornament and natural proportions, no rotation or perspective distortion. Approximately 2:1 wide output if possible. It will be overlaid above the existing menu in the game, so export only the title logo.
Background: real alpha transparency, not black, white or a drawn checkerboard. NO environment, no trees behind the letters, no UI buttons, no frame, no mockup screen, no extra icons or characters. Output one transparent PNG.
