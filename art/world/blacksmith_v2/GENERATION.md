# Isometric blacksmith

Generated with the built-in image generation tool from the previous blacksmith sprite and a screenshot of the misaligned ground grid. The prompt preserved the timber-and-stone building, tiled roof, forge, anvil and apron while rebuilding the stone base for a 2:1 dimetric projection. Transparent PNG. The in-game sprite has a small horizontal correction and anchors its lowest paving corner to the grid cell.
